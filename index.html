About

I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical Engineering at Columbia University, advised by Prof. Tanvir Ahmed Khan. I work at the boundary of compilers and hardware/software co-design.

My research spans profile-guided optimization for data center applications, power management for large-scale and heterogeneous systems, and energy efficiency of GPUs and other accelerators. Recently, I have been working on energy efficiency for distributed machine learning. My long-term goal is a general system that can optimize any combination of heterogeneous hardware for any application.

Before Columbia, I received my B.Sc. (ranked 1st in class) and M.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from BUET, where I also served as a Lecturer for three years.

Earlier papers list me as Mohammad Tawhidul Hasan Bhuiyan.
